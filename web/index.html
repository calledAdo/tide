$TIDE · THE MECHANIC

HOW IT WORKS

01

THE SEA IS THE SUPPLY

The sea is unminted supply. It starts at 700,000 fish and grows once per season.

02

BOATS FISH, FUEL BURNS

Holders stake TIDE as boats. Catch is minted and fishing fuel is burned.

03

OVERFISHING IS PUBLIC

A boat may fish harder than its fair share, but the excess is recorded as a public indictment for the assembly cycle.

04

THE ASSEMBLY CAN EXILE

Every third season, eligible boats can vote to ostracize. A name written by at least 40% of eligible boats is exiled for 10 seasons.

05

NO ADMIN, NO SETTER

There is no owner, administrator, parameter setter, or cron job. Any call or tick settles elapsed seasons, up to 40 per call.
